Data Representation and Pixelation

Learning Objective: Students will understand that computers do not process visual images; instead, they represent all visual data using numbers.

Applications: Code.org

Activity: Did you know computers don't actually see pictures or letters? They only see numbers! Today, we will use Code.org to make our own pixel art. You will type in special number codes to tell the computer exactly what colors to use and where to put them.

Binary - The Power of On and Off

Learning Objective: Students will explore how base-2 binary numbers use "on" (1) and "off" (0) states to store information.

Applications: Canva Education

Activity: We are diving into Binary code—the language of 1s and 0s! Computers use physical switches that are either "on" (1) or "off" (0) to store everything. You will use Canva to create an awesome poster showing how the pixel art you made last week is actually stored as binary numbers inside the computer.
